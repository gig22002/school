\textbf{Handshake Game}

2PP: Two parties A \& B.\\
A sends $A,\ N_A$ (nonce of A)\\
B sends back $N_A,N_B,MAC_k(N_A,N_B,B\rightarrow A, 2)$\\
A then sends $MAC_k(N_A,N_B,A\rightarrow B, 3)$
Nonce is randomly generated for each interaction, counter protects against replay\\
Note: hash functions and their keys are always known, attackers can freely compute\\
Don't encrypt within a MAC, because the information can be leaked

\textbf{Euler totient}

$l>0$\\
$\Phi(p^l)=p^l-p^{l-1}$ $\leftarrow$ want things in this form\\
$\Phi(A*B)=\Phi(A)\Phi(B)$

$\Phi(80)=\Phi(2^4*5)$, Prime Factorization\\
$\Phi(80)+\Phi(2729)=\Phi(2^4*5)+\Phi(2^3*11*31)$\\
$\Phi(2^4)*\Phi(5)+\Phi(2^3)*\Phi(11)*\Phi(31)$\\

\textbf{Homomorphism (ElGamal)}

The multiplication of two ciphertexts is equal to the encryption of the multiplication of its plaintexts
